About Mahalakshmi

Awakening Inner Wisdom, Nurturing Wholeness

Mahalakshmi Rajagopal is an intuitive Energy Healer, holistic wellness professional and spiritual guide who helps people restore harmony across body, mind, emotions and soul.

Drawing on over two decades in mental health, counselling and holistic healing, she brings ancient energy wisdom together with intuitive guidance. She believes that physical discomfort, emotional distress and recurring life challenges often arise from energetic imbalances, unresolved emotions, subconscious patterns and a disconnection from one’s authentic self.

Through chakra balancing, aura reading, intuitive energy scanning, angel card guidance and divine healing practices, she gently helps people find their energetic blockages and restore the natural flow of life force energy. Each session is a sacred space where healing unfolds through awareness, compassion and the body’s own innate intelligence.

Her guidance helps people release limiting beliefs, heal emotional wounds, awaken inner clarity, strengthen their intuition and align with their soul’s purpose.
